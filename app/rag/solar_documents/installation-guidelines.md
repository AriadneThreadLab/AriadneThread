# Photovoltaic installation guidelines

Installation guidance covers mechanical mounting, electrical safety, and
workmanship. This note summarises those themes for grounding. It is not a
site-specific method statement and it does not compute structural loads.

## Mechanical and electrical themes

Guidelines address rail and clamp compatibility with the chosen module, roof
or ground-anchor practice, cable management, earthing, and access for
maintenance. They distinguish rooftop attachments from ground-mounted tables.
They also stress that local building codes and the module installation manual
prevail over generic narrative. None of those themes is a solar-resource
calculation.

## Why installation notes appear in RAG

Users ask whether a roof or a field can host an array. The methodological
answer is that installability depends on structure, waterproofing, and
electrical rules, which are documented qualitatively here. Load calculations,
pull-out tests, and string sizing are engineering tasks. SolarDSS may later
consume structural or layout inputs; this corpus must not invent them.

## Separation from indicators

Cite this document when explaining installation practice. Keep terrain slope,
irradiance, and yield in the deterministic analytical partition when the
engine has produced them. Never read an energy figure out of installation
prose.
